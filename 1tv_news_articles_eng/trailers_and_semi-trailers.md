Trailers and semi-trailers are banned on Nakerala Pass and Sachkhere-Uzunta-Shkmeri-Zudali road due to snow
09:50, 10.04.2026
 
Due to snowfall, the movement of trailers and semi-trailers is temporarily prohibited on the section of the Kutaisi (Motsameta)-Tkibuli-Ambrolauri national road between kilometres 39 and 60 (Nakerala Pass), as well as on the Sachkhere (Sareki)-Uzunta-Shkmeri-Zudali road.

According to the Roads Department of the Ministry of Infrastructure of Georgia, the movement of other types of vehicles remains unrestricted.
