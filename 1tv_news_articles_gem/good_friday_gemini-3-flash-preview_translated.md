# Good Friday at the Holy Trinity Cathedral [Photo]
14:36, 10.04.2026
 
Today is Good Friday – the most solemn day of Holy Week. The Orthodox Church commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus Christ, His removal from the cross, and His burial.

The Locum Tenens of the Patriarchal Throne, Metropolitan Shio, is performing the Royal Hours, Vespers, and the bringing out of the Shroud (Epitaphios) at the Holy Trinity Patriarchal Cathedral.

On Good Friday, the Divine Liturgy is not celebrated in churches because, according to the Gospel, the Lord sacrificed Himself to redeem humanity from their sins. During the service, the Royal Hours are performed, during which the Gospel accounts of the Lord's Passion are read.
