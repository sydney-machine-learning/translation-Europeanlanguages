**Free metro and bus travel in Tbilisi for Easter holiday**

14:24, 10.04.2026

Due to the Easter holiday, the Tbilisi Metro will operate until 04:00 on April 12, and travel will be free from 23:00 on April 11 until 04:00 on April 12. The information is reported by the Tbilisi City Hall.

In addition, by decision of the capital’s City Hall, nine temporary bus routes have been introduced for April 12–13.

“Buses will transport passengers from 08:00 to 20:00 to the Mukhatgverdi, Vashlijvari, Kukia, New Kukia, Makhatta, Saburtalo, Ghrmaghele, Veli, and Varketili cemeteries.

The buses will run from the metro stations ‘Marjanishvili,’ ‘Delisi,’ and ‘Sarajishvili,’ from the area near ‘Varketili,’ as well as from the upper exit of ‘Didube,’ Station Square (opposite ‘Tbilisi Central’), Besarion Chichinadze Street (opposite building No. 25), and from Varketili III massif — from the intersection of Shuamta and Abashvili streets.

Citizens will be able to use buses on these routes free of charge for two days,” the Tbilisi City Hall said.
