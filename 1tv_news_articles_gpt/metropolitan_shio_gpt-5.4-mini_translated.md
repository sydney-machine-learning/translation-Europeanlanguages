**Metropolitan Shio Mujiri — If we turn to the Lord for help, and if our desire is genuine and strong, He will surely come with His grace, correct us, and cleanse us**  
16:47, 10.04.2026

The Lord expects from us that we compel our soul, burdened and wounded by our sins, to turn to Him for salvation and mercy. Our correction, our inner transformation, is possible only through God. We cannot do this by our own strength, said the locum tenens of the Patriarchal Throne, Metropolitan Shio of Senaki and Chkhorotsku (Mujiri), during a sermon at the Holy Trinity Cathedral, where the Rite of the Descent from the Cross was recently performed.

According to Metropolitan Shio, “if our desire is genuine, strong, and serious, the Lord will surely come with His grace, correct us, and cleanse us.”

“Through the magnificent and ancient liturgy of our Church, composed by the great Holy Fathers, renowned hymnographers, the finest poets, thinkers, and wise men, we have the opportunity not only to learn from the Gospel about the events connected with the Lord Jesus Christ, but also, through the liturgy, to become participants in these events. That is why today we brought out the Holy Shroud, and we are all participants in this event. Those gathered here are not the ones who recently cried out, ‘Crucify Him, crucify Him,’ but rather those who say to the Lord, ‘Forgive us, O Lord.’ We are instructed by the Church; we know the teaching of the Church, that with our sins we crucify the Lord again and again. He came to earth, became incarnate, suffered, and died in order to free us from the bondage of sin, from the burden of sin. Who was the first person whom the Lord freed from this burden? — one of the crucified thieves, the wise thief, who so deeply felt the sufferings of the innocent Savior that he confessed his sins and turned to the Savior in repentance from the cross. The Lord also expects this from us: that we compel our soul, burdened and wounded by our sins, to turn to God for salvation and mercy. Many of us, and some even for years, try on our own to fix something in our soul, in our life, without God, by our own strength. This is a futile attempt. It is only a proud, foolish attempt, because our correction, our inner transformation, is possible only through God. We cannot do this by our own strength. We may slightly change our outward behavior, but it is beyond our reach to change inwardly, as the Lord says, ‘Without Me you can do nothing.’ Dear brothers and sisters, the Lord desires our correction and purification. I will remind you: once a sick man came to Him and said, ‘Lord, if You are willing, You can cleanse me.’ What did the Lord answer? — ‘I am willing; be cleansed.’ This is what the Lord desires. The will of man and God coincided, and that man was healed. So our correction and purification are desired by the Lord even more than we desire them. If our desire is genuine, strong, and serious, and we turn to the Lord for help, He will surely come with His grace, correct us, and cleanse us. May God bless you and strengthen you. Let us pray to the Lord to grant us strength and help so that we may walk the path of truth, the path of light, and with Christ. Amen,” said Metropolitan Shio.
