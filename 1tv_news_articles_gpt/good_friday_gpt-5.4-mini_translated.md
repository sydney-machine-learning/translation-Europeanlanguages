**Red Friday at the Holy Trinity Cathedral [Photo]**  
14:36, 10.04.2026

Today is Red Friday — the most solemn day of Holy Week. The Orthodox Church commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus Christ, His removal from the Cross, and His burial.

The locum tenens of the Patriarchal Throne, Metropolitan Shio, is officiating the Great Hours, Vespers, and the procession with the Shroud at the Patriarchal Cathedral of the Holy Trinity.

On Red Friday, the Divine Liturgy is not celebrated in churches, because, according to the Gospel, the Lord offered Himself as a sacrifice to save humanity from sin. During the service, the Royal Hours are performed, during which the Gospel about the Lord’s Passion is read.
