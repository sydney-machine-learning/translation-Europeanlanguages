Good Friday at the Trinity Cathedral [Photo]
14:36, 10.04.2026

Today is Good Friday – the most solemn day of Holy Week. The Orthodox Church commemorates the crucifixion, removal from the cross and burial of Jesus Christ.

Metropolitan Shio, the Patriarchal Throne, celebrates Great Hours, Vespers and the Intercession of the Transfiguration at the Holy Trinity Cathedral.

On Good Friday, no liturgy is celebrated in churches, because, according to the Gospel, the Lord offered Himself as a sacrifice to redeem people from their sins. During the service, the Royal Hour is completed, during which the Gospel about the Passion of the Lord is read.
