Water Polo | Georgian National Team Defeats Turkey and Takes a Big Step Towards the World Cup #1TVSPORT
13:20, 10.04.2026

The Georgian national water polo team held the round of 16 match of the World Cup qualifying stage against Turkey and won 19:12. This was a worthy revenge for the defeat against the same team in the group stage.

With this win, the Georgians advanced to the quarterfinals, where their opponent will be determined later (the winner between Canada and Ukraine). To qualify for the World Cup, the Georgian national team needs to win two more matches – the quarterfinals and the semifinals. The two finalists will receive a ticket to the World Cup superfinal.

The 2026 Water Polo World Cup will be held in Australia on July 22-26.
