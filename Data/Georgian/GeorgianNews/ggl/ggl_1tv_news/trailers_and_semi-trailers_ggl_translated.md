The movement of trailers and semi-trailers is prohibited on the Nakerala Pass and on the Sachkhere (Sareki)-Uzunta-Skhmeri-Zudali road
09:23, 10.04.2026

Due to snow, the movement of trailers and semi-trailers is temporarily prohibited on the section of the Kutaisi (Motsameta)-Tkibuli-Ambrolauri road of internal state importance, km39-km60 (Nakerala Pass) and on the Sachkhere (Sareki)-Uzunta-Skhmeri-Zudali road.

The information is released by the Roads Department of the Ministry of Infrastructure of Georgia.

According to them, the movement of other types of vehicles is free.
