The rite of transfiguration was performed at the Trinity Cathedral [Photo]
16:00, 10.04.2026

The traditional rite of transfiguration was performed at the Trinity Cathedral. The service symbolically depicts the burial and mourning of the crucified Jesus Christ.

The rite was performed today by the Patriarchal Throne, Metropolitan of Senaki and Chkhorotsku Shio (Mujiri) together with the hierarchs.

Today is Good Friday, the most solemn day of Holy Week. The Orthodox Church commemorates the crucifixion, removal from the cross, and burial of Jesus Christ.

No services are held in churches on Good Friday, because, according to the Gospel, the Lord offered himself as a sacrifice to save people from their sins. During the service, the Holy Hour ends, during which the Gospel of the Lord's Passion is read.

Believers dye eggs red on this day and also bake Easter cakes, because, according to the Gospel, the crucifixion of Jesus Christ took place on the Jewish Passover holiday.
