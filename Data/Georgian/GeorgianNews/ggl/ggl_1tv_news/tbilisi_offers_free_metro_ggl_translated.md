Tbilisi Metro and Bus Rides Will Be Free on Easter
14:24, 10.04.2026

During Easter, Tbilisi Metro will operate until 04:00 on April 12, and travel will be free from 23:00 on April 11 to 04:00 on April 12. The information is released by the Tbilisi City Hall.

In addition, by decision of the City Hall, nine temporary bus routes have been established for April 12-13.

“Buses will transport passengers from 08:00 to 20:00 to Mukhatgverdi, Vashlijvari, Kukiya, Akhali Kukiya, Makhata, Saburtalo, Grmageli, Veli and Varketili cemeteries.

Buses will operate from the following metro stations: “Marjanishvili”, “Delisi”, “Sarajishvili”, “Varketili” from the surrounding area, as well as from the upper exit of “Didube”, from the station square (directly from Tbilisi Central), from Besarion Chichinadze Street (directly from Building N25) and from Varketili III Massif – from the intersection of Shuamta and Abashvili Streets.

Citizens will be able to use the buses on these routes free of charge for two days,” the Tbilisi City Hall information states.
