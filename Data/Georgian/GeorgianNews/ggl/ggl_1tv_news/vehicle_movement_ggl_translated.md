Vehicle movement prohibited on the Gudauri (Posta)-Kobi section
09:21, 10.04.2026

Vehicle movement is prohibited on the Gudauri (Posta)-Kobi section, – the Roads Department of the Ministry of Infrastructure of Georgia has announced.

As stated in the information of the Roads Department, the avalanche danger level is high on the mentioned section.

“According to the Roads Department of the Ministry of Infrastructure of Georgia, according to the National Environmental Agency, the avalanche danger level is high on the Gudauri (Posta)-Kobi section of the Mtskheta-Stepantsminda-Larsi highway of international importance, which is why all types of vehicle movement on the said section are temporarily prohibited.

According to the National Environmental Agency, vehicle movement on the Mleta-Gudauri section will be carried out in a non-stop mode,” the information says.
