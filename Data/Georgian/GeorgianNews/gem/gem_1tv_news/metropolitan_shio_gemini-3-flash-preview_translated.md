**Metropolitan Shio Mujiri - If we turn to the Lord for help, and if our desire is genuine and strong, He will surely come with His grace, correct us, and cleanse us**
16:47, 10.04.2026
 
The Lord expects us to compel our wounded souls, burdened by our sins, to turn to Him for salvation and mercy. Only God can achieve our correction and internal change. We cannot do this by our own strength – this was stated by the Locum Tenens of the Patriarchal Throne, Metropolitan Shio (Mujiri) of Senaki and Chkhorotsqu, during his sermon at the Holy Trinity Cathedral, where the rite of the Burial of Christ (Taking Down from the Cross) was recently performed.

According to Metropolitan Shio, "If our desire is genuine, strong, and serious, the Lord will surely come with His grace, correct us, and cleanse us."

"Through the glorious and ancient divine services of our Church, which were composed by the great Holy Fathers, famous hymnographers, the best poets, thinkers, and wise men, we have the opportunity not only to learn about the events related to the Lord Jesus Christ from the Gospel but to become participants in these events through the liturgy. Therefore, today we have brought out the Holy Shroud (*Gardanokhshna*), and we are all participants in this event. Those gathered here—we who are gathered—are not those who a short while ago shouted 'Crucify Him, crucify Him,' but those who say to the Lord, 'Forgive us, Lord.' We are instructed by the Church; we know the Church's teaching that through our sins we crucify the Lord again and again. He came to earth, became man, suffered, and died to free us from the captivity and burden of sins. 

Who was the first person the Lord freed from this burden? One of the crucified thieves, the Wise Thief, who felt the sufferings of the innocent Savior so deeply that he confessed his sins and turned to the Savior from the cross with repentance. The Lord expects exactly this from us: that we compel our wounded souls, burdened by our sins, to turn to God for salvation and mercy. Many of us, some even for years, try independently to correct something in our souls and in our lives without God, by our own strength. This is a futile effort. It is only a proud, foolish attempt, because only God can achieve our correction and internal change. We cannot do this by our own strength. We may slightly change our behavior externally, but it is unattainable for us to change internally, as the Lord says: 'Without Me, you can do nothing.' 

Dear brothers and sisters, the Lord desires our correction and cleansing. I remind you, once a sick man came to Him and addressed Him: 'Lord, if You are willing, You can make me clean.' What did the Lord reply? 'I am willing; be cleansed.' The Lord wants this. The will of man and God coincided, and that man was healed. Thus, the Lord desires our correction and cleansing even more than we do. If our desire is genuine, strong, and serious, and we turn to the Lord for help, He will surely come with His grace, correct us, and cleanse us. May God bless you and strengthen you. Let us pray to the Lord to give us strength and help to walk the path of truth and light, and to be with Christ, Amen," stated Metropolitan Shio.
