**Traffic is prohibited on the Gudauri (Posta)-Kobi section**
09:21, 10.04.2026
 
Traffic is prohibited on the Gudauri (Posta)-Kobi section – this information is released by the Roads Department of the Ministry of Regional Development and Infrastructure of Georgia.

As noted in the information from the Roads Department, the level of avalanche hazard on the mentioned section is high.

"According to the Roads Department of the Ministry of Regional Development and Infrastructure of Georgia, based on information from the National Environmental Agency, the level of avalanche hazard on the Gudauri (Posta)-Kobi section of the Mtskheta-Stepantsminda-Larsi international highway is high, due to which traffic for all types of vehicles is temporarily prohibited on the mentioned section.

According to the National Environmental Agency, traffic on the Mleta-Gudauri section will be carried out in a non-stop movement regime," the statement says.
