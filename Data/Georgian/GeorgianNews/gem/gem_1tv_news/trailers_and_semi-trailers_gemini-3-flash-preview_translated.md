**Traffic for trailers and semi-trailers is prohibited on the Nakerala Pass and the Sachkhere (Sareki)-Uzunta-Shkmeri-Zudali road**

09:23, 10.04.2026

Due to snowfall, traffic for trailers and semi-trailers is temporarily prohibited on the km39-km60 section (Nakerala Pass) of the Kutaisi (Motsameta)-Tkibuli-Ambrolauri secondary road and on the Sachkhere (Sareki)-Uzunta-Shkmeri-Zudali road.

The information is released by the Roads Department of the Ministry of Regional Development and Infrastructure of Georgia.

According to the department, movement for all other types of vehicles is unrestricted.
