**Vakhtang Tsintsadze - ADB report highlights Georgia's attractive business environment and ongoing infrastructure projects, including port and railway sectors**
15:29, 10.04.2026
 
The Asian Development Bank (ADB) has published the *Asian Development Outlook* report, according to which economic growth in 2025 was quite high at 7.5 percent. For 2026, against the backdrop of ongoing conflicts, the ADB expects economic growth in Georgia to be 5.5 percent, which is an improvement of 0.5 percentage points compared to the previous year's forecast, stated Vakhtang Tsintsadze, Deputy Minister of Economy and Sustainable Development.

As the Deputy Minister noted, the ADB focuses on the reduction of the current account deficit to a record low level, as well as the record high level of international reserves.

According to Vakhtang Tsintsadze, in 2026, the ADB expects the average inflation rate in Georgia to be 3.8 percent, which is a decrease compared to the previous year.

"The Asian Development Bank also emphasizes that Georgia strives to become a trade, logistics, and transport hub. In this regard, attention is focused on numerous reforms that facilitate, among other things, the improvement of customs and other procedures. The bank also highlights the infrastructural achievements Georgia has in both the port and railway sectors," said Vakhtang Tsintsadze.

According to the Deputy Minister of Economy, the ADB report highlights Georgia's attractive business environment, focusing on the free trade agreements Georgia holds, as well as the mechanisms that ensure investor protection. This ultimately increases the trust and attractiveness of Georgia for both local and international investors.
