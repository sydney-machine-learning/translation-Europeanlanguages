Water Polo | Georgia’s national team defeated Turkey and took a big step toward the World Cup #1TVSPORT  
13:20, 10.04.2026

The Georgian national water polo team played its Round of 16 match in the World Cup qualification stage against Turkey and won 19:12. This was a worthy revenge for the loss to the same team in the group stage.

With this victory, the Georgians advanced to the quarterfinals, where their opponent will be determined later (the winner of Canada-Ukraine). To qualify for the World Cup, Georgia still needs to win two more matches — the quarterfinal and the semifinal. The two finalists will earn spots in the World Cup Super Final.

The 2026 Water Polo World Cup will be held in Australia from July 22 to 26.
