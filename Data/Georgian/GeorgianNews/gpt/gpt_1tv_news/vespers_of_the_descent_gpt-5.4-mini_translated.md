The Rite of the Taking Down from the Cross Was Performed at the Holy Trinity Cathedral [Photo]  
16:00, 10.04.2026

The traditional rite of the Taking Down from the Cross was performed at the Holy Trinity Cathedral. The service symbolically reflects the burial and lamentation of Jesus Christ, who was crucified.

Today, the rite was conducted by the Locum Tenens of the Patriarchal Throne, Metropolitan Shio of Senaki and Chkhorotsku (Mujiri), together with the bishops.

Today is Good Friday, the most solemn day of Holy Week. The Orthodox Church commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus Christ, His taking down from the cross, and His burial.

On Good Friday, no liturgy is celebrated in churches, because, according to the Gospel, the Lord offered Himself as a sacrifice to redeem humanity from sin. During the service, the Royal Hours are performed, during which the Gospel about the Lord’s Passion is read.

On this day, the faithful dye eggs red and also bake paskha, because, according to the Gospel, the crucifixion of Jesus Christ took place on the Jewish Passover feast.
