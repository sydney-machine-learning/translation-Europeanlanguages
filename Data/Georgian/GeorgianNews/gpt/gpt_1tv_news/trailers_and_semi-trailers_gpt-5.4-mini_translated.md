Trailer and semi-trailer vehicle traffic is prohibited on the Nakerala Pass and the Sachkhere (Sareki)-Uzunta-Shkmeri-Zudali road  
09:23, 10.04.2026

Due to snowfall, the movement of trailer and semi-trailer vehicles is temporarily prohibited on the km39-km60 section of the domestic road of state importance Kutaisi (Motsameta)-Tkibuli-Ambrolauri (Nakerala Pass) and on the Sachkhere (Sareki)-Uzunta-Shkmeri-Zudali road.

The information is reported by the Roads Department of the Ministry of Infrastructure of Georgia.

According to them, the movement of all other types of vehicles is unrestricted.
